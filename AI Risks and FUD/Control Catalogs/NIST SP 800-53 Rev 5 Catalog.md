# NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Control Catalog

Back to [[Addressing Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt in the Security and Safety of Artificial Intelligence Models|main note]] · [[RQ3 Control Coverage]] · [[RQ3.1 Minimum Control Set]]

Extracted with AI assistance on Nov. 4, 2026 from NIST's Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool (CPRT) catalog service, SP 800-53 Release 5.1.1 element data (https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/cprt/catalog#/cprt/framework/version/SP_800_53_5_1_1/home). Control statements only; discussion text and related-control lists are omitted. Withdrawn controls and enhancements are kept with the mark **W** so that cross-references resolve. Release 5.2.0 (Aug. 2025) added SA-15(13), SA-24, and SI-2(7) and broadened SI-7(12); those are not in this extract [276]. Source for citation [15]. NIST publications are in the public domain in the United States.

**Counts.** 1,193 entries: 323 base controls and 870 enhancements, of which 182 are withdrawn. Identifiers are written without leading zeros (AC-3(2)), as the paper cites them; CPRT writes AC-03(02).
