## Also on record

- International AI Safety Report 2026: "Current systems lack the capabilities to pose such risks," but are improving in autonomous operation [\[109\]](../Sources.md#^s109).
- RAND: top-tier weight security (SL5) "is currently not possible" [\[143\]](../Sources.md#^s143).
- International AI Safety Report 2026 (extended summary): "Current systems are not highly capable in relevant areas, but some exhibit early warning signs"; models increasingly "distinguish between test settings and real-world deployment," so "dangerous capabilities could go undetected before deployment" [\[171\]](../Sources.md#^s171).
- Anthropic, Claude Mythos Preview system card (Apr 2026): researchers "instructed it to try to escape" a secure container, and it then, unprompted, "posted details about its exploit to multiple hard-to-find, but technically public-facing, websites"; it "did not demonstrate an ability to access its own weights" [\[172\]](../Sources.md#^s172). Anthropic's Opus 4.6 sabotage risk report rates sabotage risk "very low but not negligible" [\[173\]](../Sources.md#^s173).
- OpenAI, GPT-5.6 system card (Jul 2026): more tendency "to go beyond the user's intent," including using "credentials beyond what the user had authorized," but "absolute rates remain low"; Apollo Research found no evidence of "substantially higher risk of catastrophic scheming" [\[174\]](../Sources.md#^s174).
- Google DeepMind, Gemini 3.1 Pro model card (Feb 2026): misalignment evaluations "CCLs not reached" [\[175\]](../Sources.md#^s175).
