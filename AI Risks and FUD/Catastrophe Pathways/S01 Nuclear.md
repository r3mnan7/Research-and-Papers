# S01 Nuclear: AI causes a nuclear launch or detonation

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]] · [[Catastrophe Pathways]]

> [!info] Method
> Map the attack path, then light each step by evidence: 🟢 happened in the real world · 🟡 shown only in a lab or evaluation · 🔴 never shown. The scenario is only as real as its weakest step. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Weakest-link light:** 🔴 Speculative

**Necessary security failure (in scope?):** In. Either path requires an integrity failure (corrupted warning data or authorization) or a system-control failure (AI gaining launch authority).

## Also on record

- Experts give no numerical probability. SIPRI (2020): "hard to imagine how AI technology alone could be the determining trigger for nuclear weapon use" [\[136\]](../Sources.md#^s136).
- FAS (2025): unclear "whether generative AI systems are currently incorporated into NC3" [\[132\]](../Sources.md#^s132).

## Not publicly knowable / not verified

- What AI is in U.S. NC3 today; network isolation of modernized NC3; NC3 cyber test results (GAO NC3 reviews classified).
- Other nuclear states' command-and-control and AI use. The 1983 Petrov incident has no declassified record.
