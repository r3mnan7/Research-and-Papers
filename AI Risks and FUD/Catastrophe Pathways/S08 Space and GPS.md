# S08 Space and GPS: disruption of satellites or navigation causes catastrophe

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]] · [[Catastrophe Pathways]]

> [!info] Method
> Map the attack path, then light each step by evidence: 🟢 happened in the real world · 🟡 shown only in a lab or evaluation · 🔴 never shown. The scenario is only as real as its weakest step. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Weakest-link light:** 🔴 Speculative as catastrophe. Disruption is 🟢, without AI.

**Necessary security failure (in scope?):** In. The cyber path (ground segment, terminals) requires a security failure. Jamming and spoofing are electronic warfare, an availability failure of the signal rather than hacking.

## Attack path

| # | Step | Light | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cyberattack on satellite ground segment or user terminals | 🟢 | Viasat KA-SAT, Feb 24, 2022: a ground-based intrusion exploited "a misconfiguration in a VPN appliance," then management commands "overwrote key data in flash memory" on residential modems; tens of thousands of customers across Europe lost service and Viasat shipped nearly 30,000 replacement modems. Viasat found no compromise of the satellite or its ground infrastructure, and no firmware tampering [\[202\]](../Sources.md#^s202). |
