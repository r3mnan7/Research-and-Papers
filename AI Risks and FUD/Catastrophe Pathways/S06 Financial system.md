# S06 Financial system: AI or AI-enabled cyber triggers a market crash or crisis

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]] · [[Catastrophe Pathways]]

> [!info] Method
> Map the attack path, then light each step by evidence: 🟢 happened in the real world · 🟡 shown only in a lab or evaluation · 🔴 never shown. The scenario is only as real as its weakest step. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Weakest-link light:** 🔴 Speculative

**Necessary security failure (in scope?):** In. The cyber path (compromise of a shared AI model or provider, or a systemic node) is analyzed. Outside scope: the AI-herding path, which needs no security failure.

## Attack path

| # | Step | Light | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Compromise a shared AI model or provider used across many firms | 🔴 | Regulators flag "common AI models with shared cyber vulnerabilities across systemic firms" [\[185\]](../Sources.md#^s185) and provider concentration [\[186\]](../Sources.md#^s186). No exploitation documented. |
| 2 | Cyber event at a systemic financial node | 🟢 | Ransomware at ICBC Financial Services, Nov 2023: ICBC said it "disconnected and isolated impacted systems" and cleared the affected Treasury trades (company statement, via press); Treasury Secretary Yellen: "We've not seen an impact on the Treasury market" (to reporters, via press). The reported $9B settlement exposure rests on anonymous sources. IMF: cyber incidents "have thus far not been systemic" [\[187\]](../Sources.md#^s187). |
| 3 | Propagation into a disorderly crash | 🟢 | Happened without cyber or AI: the May 6, 2010 Flash Crash, intraday lows down 9–10% [\[188\]](../Sources.md#^s188). |
| 4 | Defeat circuit breakers and recovery, causing a sustained crisis | 🔴 | In 2010 prices largely recovered within the hour and a CME pause halted the slide [\[188\]](../Sources.md#^s188). Market-wide circuit breakers and limit up-limit down bands now apply [\[189\]](../Sources.md#^s189). |

## Also on record

- SEC Chair Gensler (2023): AI "may play a central role in the after-action reports of a future financial crisis" [\[190\]](../Sources.md#^s190).

## Not publicly knowable / not verified

- No regulator report on ICBC; the company and Treasury statements are known through press quotation, and the $9B figure is from anonymous sources.
- Non-U.S. market controls not checked.
