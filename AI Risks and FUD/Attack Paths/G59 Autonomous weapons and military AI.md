# G59 Autonomous weapons and military AI

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]]

Path, lights, and scope call produced with AI assistance under the fixed method in the screening log. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Harm (as claimed):** Lethal autonomous weapons kill without meaningful human control, including unlawful or civilian deaths. AI-enabled targeting increases harm. Military AI creates national-security and escalation risk, up to inadvertent war.

**Path (LAWS):**

| # | Step | Light | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | States field weapons capable of autonomous target engagement | 🟢 | UN Panel of Experts on Libya, S/2021/229 (8 Mar 2021), para 63, via ICRC Casebook https://casebook.icrc.org/node/21244: "programmed to attack targets without requiring data connectivity," naming the Kargu-2 ("fire, forget and find") |
| 2 | The system engages humans without human control | 🟢 | Same report describes combat engagements by LAWS. Whether autonomous mode was actually used is not independently confirmed (see Gaps). |
| 3 | The engagement is unlawful or misdirected (civilian harm, IHL violation) attributable to autonomy | 🔴 | No primary-documented case found |

**Path (AI-enabled targeting, human in the loop):**

| # | Step | Light | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI decision-support systems generate or prioritise targets in real operations | 🟢 | IDF, "The IDF's Use of Data Technologies in Intelligence Processing," 18 Jun 2024 (updated 19 Oct 2026), https://www.idf.il/210062: Habsora "suggests to the intelligence analyst to focus his or her research on certain physical objects," in operational use since 2021. Ukraine MoD, 23 Sep 2024, https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/12-000-enemy-targets-are-detected-by-the-ukrainian-military-weekly: the Avengers platform lets forces "automatically detect 12,000 units of enemy vehicles/equipment weekly." NGA: "NGA Maven is now a program of record that is being employed by real-world combat commanders" (https://info.nga.mil/news/NGA_director_discusses_agencys_future_with_GEOINT_.html, undated). |
| 2 | Humans approve AI-generated targets with limited verification | 🔴 (allegation only) | OHCHR (UN Special Rapporteurs), 15 Apr 2024, https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2024/04/gaza-un-experts-deplore-use-purported-ai-commit-domicide-gaza-call: "If proven true," citing "lowered human due diligence." UN Secretary-General, 5 Apr 2024: "deeply troubled by reports." Denied by the IDF (same page: a Habsora suggestion "cannot be solely relied upon to identify a target without being thoroughly explored by the intelligence analyst") and by CENTCOM (Adm. Cooper, 11 Mar 2026, video on @CENTCOM; wording via DefenseScoop): "Humans will always make final decisions." |
| 3 | Wrongful strikes attributable to the AI-assisted process | 🔴 (allegation only) | Minab school strike, Iran, 28 Feb 2026: the Pentagon says "Our investigation remains ongoing" (as quoted by CGTN, 20 Sep 2026); reports of "overreliance on artificial-intelligence tools" rest on anonymous sources. DoD's civilian-casualty report on the 2–3 Feb 2024 Al Qaim strike, https://policy.defense.gov/Portals/11/Documents/CHMR/TAB%20B-2024_1057_Report_final.pdf, does not mention AI. |

**Path (maximal: escalation / national security):**

| # | Step | Light | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI is integrated into military or diplomatic decision-making | 🟢 | DoD Directive 3000.09 (25 Jan 2023), https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodd/300009p.PDF, governs fielded autonomy: "appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force" |
| 2 | AI recommendations escalate conflict | 🟡 | Rivera et al., arXiv 2401.03408 (FAccT 2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03408: "all five studied off-the-shelf LLMs show forms of escalation" (wargame simulation) |
| 3 | Real-world escalation or war results | 🔴 | None found |

**Weakest-link light:** LAWS 🔴; targeting 🔴; escalation 🔴

**In scope:** In. Step 2 of the LAWS path is an authorization failure when a weapon engages outside commanders' authorized parameters, and a hijacking or spoofing variant would add integrity; only these routes are analyzed. Outside scope: the misidentification and escalation routes, which need no security failure.

**Gaps:** The UN report does not confirm autonomous-mode kills. UNODA (https://disarmament.unoda.org/the-convention-on-certain-conventional-weapons/background-on-laws-in-the-ccw/) says "no commonly agreed definition" of LAWS exists, and the Secretary-General called for a binding instrument "by 2026"; This research could not verify any outcome of that call. On the targeting path, the step that would move it is a released DoD finding on the Minab strike.
