# G44 Obscene / abusive content (CSAM, NCII)

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]]

Path, lights, and scope call produced with AI assistance under the fixed method in the screening log. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Harm (as claimed):** Sexual exploitation, harassment and sextortion of real people, including children, through AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material. **Harm has occurred** per official sources.

**Path:**

| # | Step | Light | Evidence (no content details) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Offender obtains source images of a real person (often benign public photos) | 🟢 | FBI PSA I-060523-PSA, 5 Jun 2023, https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2023/PSA230605 |
| 2 | Offender gets a capable generator. Alternatives: purpose-built app, open model, or misuse of a hosted service | 🟢 | FBI PSA I-032924-PSA, 29 Mar 2024, https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA240329 cites a "web-based AI application" (Charlotte case, 40-year sentence). Ofcom, 12 Jan 2026, https://ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/ofcom-launches-investigation-into-x-over-grok-sexualised-imagery: "Grok AI chatbot account on X being used to create and share undressed images of people". |
| 3 | AI generates the abusive imagery | 🟢 | IWF 2024 data, https://www.iwf.org.uk/annual-data-insights-report-2024/data-and-insights/ai-generated-child-sexual-abuse/: 245 actionable reports, "a 380% increase on 2023". DOJ, 20 May 2024, https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/man-arrested-producing-distributing-and-possessing-ai-generated-images-minors-engaged: "CSAM generated by AI is still CSAM" (Anderegg indictment; allegations). |
| 4 | Imagery is shared or used for sextortion or harassment | 🟢 | Ofcom ("create and share"). FBI 2023 PSA on sextortion schemes. |
| 5 | Harm to victims | 🟢 | FBI 2023: "continues to receive reports from victims, including minor children". IASR 2026 lists AI misuse for "non-consensual intimate imagery" but notes "systematic data on their prevalence and severity remains limited". |

**Weakest-link light:** 🟢

**In scope:** In. Step 2 is an authorization failure (getting past a hosted service's safeguards) on the hosted-service misuse route; only that route is analyzed. Outside scope: purpose-built and open-model routes, which need no security failure; step 1 uses publicly posted images.

**Gaps:** Prevalence data is limited (IASR).
