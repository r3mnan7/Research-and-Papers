# G14 Excessive agency / privilege / credentials

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]]

Path, lights, and scope call produced with AI assistance under the fixed method in the screening log. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Harm (as claimed):** When an agent is subverted or makes an error, broad or persistent privileges let the damage reach far beyond the task. This can mean credential theft, lateral movement up to cluster-admin-level access, or bulk data theft.

**Weakest-link light:** 🟢

**In scope:** Yes. Step 1 is an authorization failure (least privilege). Step 2 is an authentication failure (credential compromise). Step 3 is an authorization failure.

**Gaps:** AWS-2025-015 (23 Jul 2025, https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/AWS-2025-015/) involved an "inappropriately scoped GitHub token" in the Amazon Q build pipeline. That was a CI credential, not an agent privilege. The Salesloft Drift OAuth theft (GTIG, 26 Aug 2025, https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/data-theft-salesforce-instances-via-salesloft-drift) fits the long-lived-token pattern, but the GTIG page does not describe Drift as an AI agent, so This research did not use it as evidence.
