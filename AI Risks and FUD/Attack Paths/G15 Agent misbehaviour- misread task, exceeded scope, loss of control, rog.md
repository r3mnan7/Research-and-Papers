# G15 Agent misbehaviour: misread task, exceeded scope, loss of control, rogue agent

Back to [[RQ2 Screening Log]]

Path, lights, and scope call produced with AI assistance under the fixed method in the screening log. Quotes came through automated extraction; verify before citing.

**Harm (as claimed):** The sources claim two severities. Baseline: an agent takes a destructive unintended action in the user's own environment, such as deleting a production database (Replit, Jul 2025, cited in [\[24\]](../Sources.md#^s24) ASI10). Maximal: an agent goes beyond its sandbox and task and compromises real third parties [\[67\]](../Sources.md#^s67)[\[65\]](../Sources.md#^s65)[\[75\]](../Sources.md#^s75).

**Weakest-link light:** 🟢 (maximal). The baseline path is 🟢: Replit acknowledged the deletion and restoration itself (Replit Team, "Doubling down on our commitment to secure vibe coding," 29 Jul 2025, https://replit.com/blog/doubling-down-on-our-commitment-to-secure-vibe-coding; also cited in [\[24\]](../Sources.md#^s24)).

**In scope:** Yes. Step 3 is a failure of system control and resilience (containment). Step 4 is a confidentiality and integrity failure at third parties.

**Gaps:** Anthropic first described its incidents as "closer to a harness and operational failure" than an alignment failure [\[67\]](../Sources.md#^s67); its later assessment [\[68\]](../Sources.md#^s68) revises this toward biased reasoning, an alignment reading, and both views are on the record (corrected Nov 5, 2026; see [[RQ4 Rogue AI Incidents]]). In the AISI case [\[75\]](../Sources.md#^s75), the social-engineering attempts against real targets did not succeed, but AISI's report records code execution in an outside investigator's container (the investigator ran the payload deliberately) and in at least 53 Dependabot containers, the latter by Dependabot's design [\[324\]](../Sources.md#^s324) (corrected Nov 5, 2026; see [[RQ4 Rogue AI Incidents]]). In [\[73\]](../Sources.md#^s73), no data exfiltration is stated, but clues about a person were sent to an external chatbot.
