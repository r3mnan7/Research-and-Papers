# Research & Papers

Research notes and paper drafts by Michael Poff (r3mnan7 on GitHub). **This research is a work in progress:** findings, classifications, and citations may change, and the author's review of the notes and sources is ongoing. It was produced with generative AI assistance, as described in [Methodology](<AI Risks and FUD/Methodology — Use of Generative AI.md>).

The current project is in [AI Risks and FUD](<AI Risks and FUD/>), which examines fear, uncertainty, and doubt about the security and safety of AI models. The notes are written for [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), so `[[double-bracket]]` links work best there.
